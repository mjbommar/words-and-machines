# Open engineering/industrial math problems where machine search + a checkable certificate is the plausible path

*Produced 2026-08-25 by an Opus 5 research agent (four parallel lanes plus its own circuit-complexity work). WebSearch quota was exhausted early; most retrieval was direct fetch of arXiv, IACR ePrint, NIST CSRC, and author pages. Reproduced verbatim with its flags. `[older]` = pre-2020 source; `[PREPRINT]` = unrefereed.*

**One result the agent verified itself, in-session.** It downloaded NIST's newest AES S-box straight-line program and evaluated it on all 256 inputs against FIPS 197: **29 AND, 95 XOR, 14 XNOR, 138 gates, zero mismatches.** About 20 lines of Python. That is the entire thesis of this report in one measurement.

## Part 1 — Circuit complexity of small functions

**P1. Multiplicative complexity of the AES S-box: 29, or lower?** Best 29 AND (+195 XOR, 4 NOT), adopted by NIST 2026-08-05; the mechanism is a schedule-aware 24-product certificate replacing 27 outer products. Lower bound: general result ≥2.32n ≈ 19 for maximally nonlinear (n,n) functions (Boyar–Find, arXiv:1407.6169). **Gap 29 vs ~13–19.** Who cares: MPC/ZK (AND count is the cost), masking, FHE. Bristol Fashion AES-128 is 6,400 AND = 200 × 32; at 29 that is **5,800 — a 9.4% cut available today, and nobody has published the updated circuit.** Beating 29: months. Optimality: needs new theory.

**P2. Minimum AND-depth of the AES S-box: 3 or 4?** Best 4 (NIST "S-Box 3"). Lower bound **3, derived in-session**: AND-depth d bounds algebraic degree by 2^d; every AES S-box coordinate has ANF degree exactly 7, so d ≥ 3. **Gap exactly 1.** Nobody has run the search; depth-3 is a strong structural constraint, so the SAT problem is *small*. Who cares: FHE. **Weeks.**

**P3. Shortest linear program for AES MixColumns: 88 or fewer?** Record chain 94→92→91→89→**88** (Jean, eprint 2026/1481, "found with the help of AI"). **No nontrivial lower bound has ever been published** (trivial: 32). Stoffelen's SAT solved 7×7 optimally and failed on 19×5. Beating 88: weeks with heuristic+LLM; optimality: new theory.

**P4. The 57-matrix NIST linear-map benchmark.** For not one of NIST's 57 matrices is optimality known; NIST's table lags (lists 92 for MixColumns). **Lowest-risk place to produce a new number.**

**P5. Gate complexity of Ascon, Keccak and other S-boxes: intervals not yet closed.** Zhang–Huang (eprint 2023/1721, Tables 9–11) publish intervals `[k1,k2]` = UNSAT at k1−1, SAT at k2: **Keccak χ₅ BGC [12,13]; RECTANGLE BGC [11,12]; PRIMATEs⁻¹ MC [7,8]** (gaps of 1); Ascon BGC [12,16], GC [12,15]. Settled: MC(Ascon)=5, MC(PRIMATEs)=7. Their Remark 1 says prior published optimality results "may cause potentially incorrect results" and had to be re-verified. **A DRAT-certified S-box optimality result would be the first of its kind and a soundness upgrade.** **Weeks. Single best fit for a bit-blast + DRAT stack.**

**P6. Minimum bit-operation count for AES-128, SHA-256, 64-bit multiply.** Osvik & Canright (eprint 2024/1076): 1338 ops/AES-128. Bristol Fashion: AES-128 6,400 AND; SHA-256 22,573 AND; **mult64 4,033 AND (schoolbook)**. These were generated by Synopsys/CBMC-GC and never minimized as circuits. No lower bounds. Recompiling AES-128 with the 29-AND S-box is a 600-gate win nobody has published.

## Part 2 — Bilinear and tensor rank

**P7. R(⟨3,3,3⟩): 19 ≤ R ≤ 23.** Laderman's 23 unmoved fifty years. **Over F₂ the lower bound is now 20** (Chengu Wang, arXiv:2603.07280, final 2026-07-30) — first improvement on Bläser 2003 at this format. Yang (arXiv:2505.09824): *"our CPD search algorithms are still too slow to resolve the rank of 3×3."* **Wang's model is the one to imitate:** ~40 minutes on a laptop, verifies in seconds, independently re-verified by a third party in a different language. Rank: years; F₂ lower-bound increments: months.

**P8. `R_F2(P_6)`: 16 or 17.** *(Best-fitting problem in this report.)* Bilinear complexity of 6-term binary polynomial multiplication. UB 17 (Montgomery 2005), LB 16 (Wang 2026). Adjacent gap-1: `R_F2(⟨2,3,4⟩) ∈ [19,20]`. Other open: P_7 [19,22], P_8 [21,26], C_8 [19,22], T_9 [21,26], ⟨3,3,4⟩ [25,29]. Encoding: 368 primary + 576 + 6336 aux; 396 XOR-equations over 16 terms — **~7k variables, ~30–60k clauses.** Hard because of symmetry, not size. Who cares: GHASH (every TLS stack/NIC), Classic McEliece/BIKE/HQC, ZK. **Weeks with a good encoding; warm up on ⟨2,3,4⟩.**

**P9. Small matmul formats: the Z₂-to-Z lifting gap.** ⟨4,5,5⟩: rank 73 over F₂ (arXiv:2312.16960) but 76 is best liftable to Z. ⟨4,4,4⟩ ≤ 47 over F₂ (AlphaTensor), ≤ 48 over Q (Dumas–Pernet–Sedoglavic, arXiv:2506.13242) — whether 47 is reachable over Q is open. Hensel lifting, not search, is the wall. Months; crowded field.

## Part 3 — Sorting networks

**P10. S(13): 44 or 45?** Known to n ≤ 12 (Harder 2020, arXiv:2012.04400, DP with Isabelle-checked lower-bound certificates). LB 44 (Van Voorhis-style, 2025). **Gap 1.** S(14) [48,51], S(15) [53,56], S(16) [57,60], S(17) [63,71]. Months.

**P11. Optimal depth for n=18: 10 or 11?** Known to n ≤ 17. Bundala–Zavodny two-layer-prefix SAT; prefix count explodes past 17. Months. **Clearest "extend a known SAT result by one" target.**

## Part 4 — Numerical analysis coefficients

**P12. Optimal stability polynomials for order p ≥ 3: certified.** Ketcheson–Ahmadia (arXiv:1201.3035): every published H_opt(s,p) for p ≥ 3 is a numerical local optimum with no proof. **Cleanest SOS instance in the report**: `|R(x)| ≤ 1` on an interval is completely characterized by Markov–Lukács; s=20 gives 16 free coefficients, PSD blocks ≤ 21. **Completely checkable both directions in exact rational arithmetic.** Who cares: RKC/ROCK/RKL integrators, cardiac electrophysiology, combustion, PyFR/Trixi.jl. **Weeks.**

**P13. Exact certification of the newest high-order RK tableaux.** Zhang's 16-stage order-10 is a floating-point object with no rigorous proof; Stepanov's 15-stage has exact coefficients in Q(√7) with no independent verification. Verify against all **1205** order conditions: weeks, falsifiable, publishable. `s_min(9)` is **12 or 13** — gap 1. `s_min(10) ≥ 13` vs 15.

**P14. Optimality certificates for SSP RK coefficients.** Is C=6 exactly optimal for explicit 10-stage order-4? No matching upper bound for any (s,4), s ≥ 5. Values came from BARON with floating tolerances; they look **exactly rational**, suggesting a clean certificate exists. Small cells: weeks.

**P15. Minimal-point cubature on the triangle, degree ≥ 15.** Degree 20: 79 points vs Stroud bound 66; degree 25: 117 vs 91. Papanicolopulos settled fully-symmetric rules to degree 14 by Gröbner basis. Who cares: deal.II, FEniCS, MFEM, Firedrake, Abaqus. Assembly cost linear in points.

## Part 5 — Floating point

**P16. Kahan's hypot: 1.5355339u proven, 1.4977u observed.** Muller–Salvy (arXiv:2405.03588, Thm 9.1): *"We do not know if the bound … is sharp."* Gap 0.0378u. Squaring kills the sqrt; binary32 reference side is exact integer arithmetic in ≤ 49 bits: **~70k variables, ~280k clauses.** Beating 1.4977u: weeks.

**P17. `DWTimesDW`: 5u² proven, 4.98u² observed.** Muller–Rideau (TOMS 2022); the 2017 predecessor published 6u² on a **wrong lemma** discovered only when Rideau translated it into Coq. Gap < 0.02u². Muller: *"the worst cases are extremely unlikely: you must build them."* Random search structurally cannot find these; SAT looks for exactly this needle. **~120k variables, ~450k clauses. Weeks; either outcome publishable.**

**P18. Complex division: (3+√5)u ≈ 5.236u proven, nothing measured in binary64.** Jeannerod–Louvet–Muller–Plet 2016: only two examples exist, both at p=11. GCC/Clang compile `_Complex` division to `__divdc3` — the compilers themselves care. First binary64 example: weeks. Smith/Baudin-Smith (what actually ships) have **no published bound at all.**

**P19. Max relative error of xⁿ by iterated multiplication, n=6.** Proven 5u; exhibited 4.7805779u (Graillat–Lefèvre–Muller, arXiv:1402.2991). Binary32 n=6 is known exactly by exhaustive search: **4.328005619u — a free ground-truth control.** ~210k variables at binary64. Weeks. *(Status verified only to 2015.)*

**P20. Hard-to-round cases: bivariate binary64 and all of binary128.** CORE-MATH: for pow, atan2, hypot "only a restricted set of worst cases has been computed." Zero non-trivial worst cases known for any binary128 transcendental (420 million core-years per binade estimated). Muller: *"Absolute necessity of formal proof! … certificate-based approach based on Q1 and Q2."* **The verification layer is squarely in scope and the field asks for it.** binary32 pow sieve: months, a real "first." SLZ certificate format: hard, tractable, unclaimed.

## Part 6 — Cryptographic arithmetic

**P21. Shortest addition chain for secp256k1 scalar inversion: is 290 optimal?** Curve25519 field inversion: 265 vs Schönhage floor 261 (gap 4); secp256k1: 290 vs 262 (gap 28). Brian Smith: *"almost certainly the case that at least a couple of the ones presented here are not optimal."* Restricted "Brauer/star chain of length 289" is SAT-shaped. Shaving 1–3 steps: months, immediately deployed (libsecp256k1, curve25519-dalek, fiat-crypto).

**P22. BLS12-381 final exponentiation: the most-executed unproved chain in production.** Hayashida–Hayasaka–Teruya 2020, improved ~10% by Nyamsi–Fouotsa–Tcheka 2025, **no optimality claim, no lower bound ever stated.** Building the missing record table: weeks, high value.

**P23. GIFT-128 optimal differential trails beyond 29 rounds.** Sun–Wang–Wang (eprint 2021/213) compact SAT encoding, public code. Optimality at round r is a single-CNF UNSAT with DRAT. **Most credible route to a DRAT-certified cryptanalytic bound.** Months.

**P24. SPECK-128 round 12; SKINNY-128 beyond 13/14.** SPECK128 optimal proven to 11 rounds; characteristics reach 20. QF_BV-native (ARX). ~10⁴ vars, 10⁵ clauses. Months.

**P25. Ascon/Keccak trail bounds — and the missing certificate format.** El Hirch et al. proved Ascon 4-round bounds in 13 (diff) / 110 (lin) CPU-days with a dedicated search; general-purpose solvers needed **6,688 CPU-days (SAT) / 3,898 (SMT+MILP)** for a weaker bound — **a 100–500× penalty.** Do not attempt the bound with a generic solver. A certificate format for dedicated trail-bound searches is unclaimed and high value. Keccak-f[1600] 6-round lightest trail 1112 is a Matryoshka lift, likely improvable.

## Part 7 — Compiler / superoptimization

**P26. Minimal SIMD shuffle sequences.** *(Best-fitting compiler target.)* L(π) unknown for AVX2, NEON, SVE, RVV; even the Cayley-graph diameter is unpublished. **Key point: a pure-permutation shuffle problem needs no bit-vector arithmetic** — model lanes as symbolic tags; each instruction is a finite-domain function; "is there a k-instruction sequence" is pure SAT of a few thousand variables. **Improvement checked by evaluating on one input of 32 distinct tags — no solver; optimality is exhaustive UNSAT at k−1 with DRAT natively.** Minotaur's authors state the exhaustive query and decline it: *"this is very heavy lifting."* **Weeks for a first real result.**

**P27. Proven-minimal instruction sequences for division by an invariant constant.** GCC's `x/255` → `(x*0x80808081)>>39`; divisors like 7 need a fixup, 5–6 instructions. Nobody claims minimality. Small QF_BV. Suggested first target: unsigned 32-bit division by 3, 5, 7, 255 on a clean RV64 integer subset. Months. Context: HieraSynth reaches 7–8-instruction exhaustive synthesis at 700-instruction sets; **no superoptimizer emits an optimality certificate.**

## Part 8 — Control and stability

**P28. Explicit Lyapunov-degree lower bounds: push 14 → 16.** Ahmadi–Jungers (arXiv:1504.03761): largest explicit counterexample is degree 14. Non-existence at degree 16 for a 2×2 pair is a **9×9 rational SDP dual ray** — the negative certificate is *smaller* than the positive one. Months.

**P29. Finiteness conjecture for pairs of 3×3 sign matrices: ~20 million cases, stated as reachable, unattempted.** Mejstrik (arXiv:2505.10178) proved 3×3 binary (15,908 cases, two days in MATLAB) and says a C implementation could do the ~20M sign-matrix cases. Per-case rationalized invariant polytope is a finite, exactly checkable object. **Strongest pure-compute play.** Months.

**P30. Maximal angle of the 4×4 copositive cone.** Known for n ≤ 3 (Yu–Zhang, ELA 2025); θ_max(COP₄) unknown, bracket [3π/4, π]. Copositivity for n=4 has a finite characterization, so the ∀ eliminates into a 20-variable QF_NRA problem. Ideal in both directions. Weeks–months.

## The structural pattern, stated once

In **every** problem above the improvement direction is checkable in milliseconds and the optimality direction is not — except in exactly three shapes: **univariate nonnegativity** (SOS complete on an interval), **SDP infeasibility with a rational dual ray**, and **small pure-SAT instances** (DRAT for free). Those are where the fast wins are.

**Limitations.** WebSearch quota exhausted early; ACM DL returns 403 so HieraSynth/SuperStack are from abstracts; Bläser's 19 verified only via secondary sources; Mejstrik's publication year unresolved; P19 status verified only to 2015; interval-matrix Hurwitz stability and the Kalman conjecture under-researched.
