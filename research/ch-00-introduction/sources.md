# Introduction sources

Research checked on 2026-08-30. A search result is only a lead. Any claim that
enters the manuscript must be checked against the opened source and added to
`book/references.bib` with the repository's verification fields.

## Primary and authoritative sources

- Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz, "Explication de l'Arithmetique Binaire" (1703),
  original scan via e-rara:
  <https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/13195954.pdf>.
- Alan M. Turing, "On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the
  Entscheidungsproblem," *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society*,
  series 2, volume 42, pages 230--265, DOI 10.1112/plms/s2-42.1.230:
  <https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1112/plms/s2-42.1.230>.
- John von Neumann, *First Draft of a Report on the EDVAC* (1945), reprinted in
  *IEEE Annals of the History of Computing*:
  <https://web.mit.edu/STS.035/www/PDFs/edvac.pdf>.
- IBM, "The IBM System/360," corporate history with launch, compatibility,
  and investment claims:
  <https://www.ibm.com/history/system-360>.
- RISC-V International, ratified ISA specifications library:
  <https://docs.riscv.org/reference/isa/>.
- Intel, Intel 64 and IA-32 Software Developer's Manuals landing page, current
  version and volume descriptions:
  <https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/developer/articles/technical/intel-sdm.html>.
- ACM, IEEE Computer Society, and AAAI, *Computer Science Curricula 2023*,
  Architecture and Organization knowledge area:
  <https://csed.acm.org/knowledge-areas/>.

## Source cautions

- IBM's System/360 page is authoritative for IBM's own history and useful for
  locating documents. Its celebratory language and "$5 billion gamble" framing
  need independent historical corroboration before use as neutral analysis.
- Current Intel and RISC-V manual revisions can change during drafting. Pin the
  exact revision used for every architectural claim.
- Do not call Leibniz the inventor of binary representation without qualifying
  the older and independent histories of binary-like systems and the narrower
  achievement the cited paper actually documents.
